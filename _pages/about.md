He is an Associate Professor at [Dalian Maritime University](http://www.dlmu.edu.cn), China, and a Junior Researcher at [Lomonosov Moscow State University](https://msu.ru), Russia. 

He holds a PhD in Physics and Mathematics from Lomonosov Moscow State University, where his doctoral research focused on ionospheric modeling and its applications to global navigation satellite systems (GNSS). His research interests include satellite navigation signals, ionospheric physics and modeling, atmospheric and space sciences, and high-precision positioning and navigation. His research particularly focuses on ionospheric total electron content (TEC), global electron content (GEC), the BeiDou Global Ionospheric Model (BDGIM), the Galileo NeQuick-G model, and advanced GNSS signal structures, including Alternative Binary Offset Carrier (AltBOC) modulation. 

Dr. Chen has extensive experience in international scientific collaboration and interdisciplinary research. He previously served as an expert consultant at Huawei's Russia Research Institute and has been actively involved in academic research and teaching at Lomonosov Moscow State University. His work integrates theoretical modeling, computational methods, and satellite-based observations to investigate ionospheric variability and improve the accuracy, reliability, and performance of global satellite navigation systems.
